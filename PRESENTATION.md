**The bucket progression vanilla never finished.**

*A vanilla-first tier ladder for buckets: a cheap wooden one, a tougher bamboo one, a permanent copper one, a versatile gold one that handles lava, a premium enchantable diamond one — and a quietly revised iron recipe.*

## Why this mod

Vanilla has exactly one bucket. You either have iron, or you have nothing — there's no early-game bucket and no reason to ever craft a second kind. Bucketry fills that one missing piece with a small, readable **tier ladder** that looks like it could have shipped with the base game: wood early, bamboo as a sturdier alternative, copper as a permanent mid-tier, gold as a versatile option that handles lava, diamond as a premium enchantable tool, and iron unchanged except for a recipe tweak.

No HUD spam, no menus, no new ores. Just buckets that behave the way you'd expect.

## Custom Enchantments

Only the **Diamond Bucket** is enchantable (Enchantability = 10). It supports three custom enchantments designed to extend fluid mechanics, available both via the Enchanting Table and Enchanted Books in the Anvil.

| Enchantment | Max Level | Effect | XP Cost / Activation | Conflict |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fluid Infinity**<br>*(Infinité de fluide)* | **III** | Allows infinite placement of fluids without emptying the bucket. | **Water:** Level I: 5 XP, Level II: 3 XP, Level III: 1 XP<br>**Lava:** Level I: 20 XP, Level II: 10 XP, Level III: 5 XP | **Conservation** |
| **Conservation**<br>*(Conservation)* | **III** | Chance to duplicate a source block when scooping (leaving the original intact).<br>• Level I: 15%<br>• Level II: 30%<br>• Level III: 45% | — | **Fluid Infinity** |
| **Thermal Shield**<br>*(Bouclier Thermique)* | **I** | Grants fire resistance and blocks lava damage while the bucket is held in either hand. | — | — |

### 🪟 Fluid Infinity *(Infinité de fluide)*
This premium enchantment allows the Diamond Bucket to place fluids infinitely. Instead of emptying the bucket, the source block is placed in the world, and a small amount of XP is consumed from the player's experience bar.
*   **XP Consumption Scaling:**
    *   **Water:** Level I consumes **5 XP**, Level II consumes **3 XP**, Level III consumes **1 XP** per placement.
    *   **Lava:** Level I consumes **20 XP**, Level II consumes **10 XP**, Level III consumes **5 XP** per placement.
*   **Utility:** Ideal for building decorative water or lava features, constructing obsidian walls, or placing fluids in creative-like ways in survival mode.

### 🌾 Conservation *(Conservation)*
Designed for resource gathering, this enchantment gives a chance to preserve the fluid source block when scooping it up, effectively duplicating the resource.
*   **Activation Chance:**
    *   **Level I:** **15%** chance of duplication.
    *   **Level II:** **30%** chance of duplication.
    *   **Level III:** **45%** chance of duplication.
*   **Utility:** Essential for multiplying valuable fluids like lava without having to travel back to the Nether or deep underground.

### 🛡️ Thermal Shield *(Bouclier Thermique)*
A powerful defensive enchantment that protects the player from extreme heat. While holding a Diamond Bucket enchanted with Thermal Shield in either hand (main-hand or off-hand), the player is granted fire immunity and ignores all fire and lava damage.
*   **Utility:** Crucial for Nether exploration, mining near lava pools, or saving yourself from accidental lava falls.

### Rules & Mechanics
*   **Mutual Exclusion:** It is impossible to combine **Fluid Infinity** and **Conservation** on the same bucket, creating a choice between infinite placement or resource duplication (similar to Mending and Infinity on bows).
*   **Enchanted Books:** All three enchantments can be obtained as Enchanted Books through enchanting, villager trading, random loot, or directly from the creative inventory, and applied using the Anvil.

## Mechanics in Detail

* **Durability & Repair:** Wood, bamboo, and gold buckets are damageable items that display the standard vanilla durability bar. They wear down with use and break when exhausted. You can **repair them by combining two damaged buckets of the same type in the crafting grid** — exactly like repairing armor or tools. Since they can hold damage values, they do not stack.
* **Permanence & Stacking:** Copper, diamond, and iron buckets have no durability, never break, and their empty forms stack up to **16** in a single inventory slot.
* **Lava Transport:** Only gold, diamond, and vanilla iron buckets are capable of holding lava. Right-clicking a lava block with wood, bamboo, or copper will have no effect.
* **Powder Snow:** Copper, gold, and diamond join iron in their ability to scoop up powder snow blocks and place them back in the world.
* **Milk:** Any empty bucket can be used to milk a cow. Drinking the milk clears all status effects. For durable tiers, milking and drinking draw from the bucket's durability pool.
* **Enchanting:** Only diamond buckets are enchantable (enchantability of 10). They support 3 unique custom enchantments (*Fluid Infinity*, *Conservation*, and *Thermal Shield*).
* **Creative Tab:** The mod includes a custom creative inventory tab called **Bucketry** that displays all bucket tiers, fluid variants, custom enchanted books (at all levels), and pre-enchanted buckets ready for testing.

## Under the Hood

- **Two loaders, no Architectury:** NeoForge **and** Fabric, each a self-contained project, logic mirrored rather than shared — a deliberate choice for the bleeding-edge 26.x toolchain.
- **Minecraft 26.2** (Mojang official names, post-deobfuscation).
- **30 language translations** included; `en_us` is canonical and the rest are full translations (French uses « seau »).
- Textures are generated from vanilla references by a committed pipeline (`tools/`), and a Python resource validator (`tests/validate.py`) gates every build.
